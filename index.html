Soulful Stories is an Odisha-based boutique photography and filmmaking studio committed to capturing weddings, portraits, and pre-wedding narratives with quiet editorial distinction.

Founded by Mithun Kumar, our visual language is shaped by the ancient stone colonnades of Bhubaneswar, the coastal dawn along the Bay of Bengal, and the rhythmic warmth of Indian family life. We approach each wedding not as an event to be directed, but as an heirloom to be preserved with artistic grace.

LEGAL & TRUST

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2025

1. Information We Collect

Soulful Stories collects only personal information provided voluntarily via our commission inquiry form and direct client communications (such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, event dates, celebration locations, and aesthetic notes). We do not use third-party advertising cookies or trackers.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Information submitted is used solely to assess date availability, respond to client commission requests, provide bespoke pricing proposals, and fulfill photographic and cinematic production contracts.

3. Data Confidentiality & Sharing

We treat your personal details, wedding itineraries, and private moments with strict confidentiality. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Image Copyright & Client Privacy

We respect family privacy. Any portfolio exhibition of client photographs or films is conducted in mutual agreement and respect for the couple's celebration. Clients may request private non-disclosure commissions upon contract signing.

5. Contact for Inquiries

If you have questions regarding our privacy practices or wish to have your inquiry information removed from our records, please contact us at stories.soulful@gmail.com.
COMMISSION AGREEMENT

Terms of Commission

Studio Practice: Soulful Stories · Mithun Kumar

1. Commission Reservation

A date is reserved exclusively upon receipt of a signed commission agreement and the initial reservation retainer. Because Soulful Stories accepts a limited number of celebrations each season, dates cannot be held tentatively without confirmation.

2. Creative Autonomy & Documentary Style

Soulful Stories is commissioned for our distinct editorial, unposed, and fine-art documentary perspective. While client ritual notes and family portraits are diligently honored, creative direction, colour-grading, and framing remain under our artistic discretion.

3. Delivery Timelines

Signature teasers and highlights are typically delivered within 2–3 weeks of your celebration. The complete high-resolution colour-graded master image archive and cinematic films require meticulous craft and are delivered within 8–12 weeks.

4. Destination & Travel Logistics

For celebrations outside Bhubaneswar and Cuttack, travel, accommodation, and local transfers for the production crew are arranged in consultation with the client.
